Privacy Policy for Salman Smart Products

Effective Date: November 2026

Salman Smart Products ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of our merchants and their customers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you install and use our Shopify application.

1. Information We Collect

When you install the App, we are automatically able to access certain types of information from your Shopify account, including your store domain and product inventory data required to manage your products.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly to provide and improve the App's functionality, such as displaying, adding, and deleting products in your Shopify store and managing app billing.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your store data to third parties. All access tokens are securely encrypted and stored solely for operating the application.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Shopify Partner support email.
